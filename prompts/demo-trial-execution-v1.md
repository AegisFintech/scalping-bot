# Registered DEMO trial execution

The immutable context records the registered CODEX or LOCAL_RANGE proposal source,
profile, source hash and exact market input. Local geometry is deterministic and
is never a provider call. A provider WAIT never falls back to another source.
This local derived proposal does not grant execution authority. Fresh market,
session, account, reconciliation, current trial, cost, dynamic risk, volume and
margin checks remain mandatory. Accepted orders retain their recorded prices,
STOP_LIMIT range, expiry and native protection across profile transitions.
