## Research priorities and completion evidence

The [recent loss review](quantitative-review-426.md) measured an older release:
62 positive outcomes out of67 still produced net -4,825.61 USD. It cannot qualify
the current release. The immediate agenda starts with questions the existing
broker evidence can answer, then adds missing external context deliberately.

| Priority | Question                                                                       | Required result                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1        | Are actual costs and rare loss sizes overwhelming small wins?                  | Current-release complete scoped cohort; actual net, fees, fill-relative TP/SL, changing volume/equity and explicit missing cost/protection evidence.               |
| 2        | Are entries becoming invalid during inference or reaching the broker too late? | Provider availability, broker acknowledgement and side-specific quote clocks; WAIT, rejection, expiry, nonfill and partial outcomes retained.                      |
| 3        | Does sampled profit giveback justify testing a faster exit?                    | Coverage and duplicate audit, executable-side marks and known closing costs; gaps/counterfactual fees stay unknown. A green sample is not an executable fill.      |
| 4        | Which breakout conditions add value beyond the baseline?                       | Separately registered trend, completed-candle confirmation, round-number, SMC and DOM hypotheses; chronological tests, all attempts and negative results retained. |
| 5        | Do event timing and session liquidity explain failures?                        | Prospectively timestamped official economic releases and broker-local spread/depth, with known historical availability; no retrospective invented news signal.     |

The [primary-source research](scalping-primary-research.md) covers cTrader price/fee
units, stop-limit nonfills, broker-local DOM and official macro-release timestamps.
Its calendar observation is context, not a new trading gate.

Each experiment needs one stated change, fixed baseline, source/data/cost identity,
trial budget and rejection criteria before evaluation. Discovery can be broad;
comparisons must remain attributable. Optimize cost-inclusive expectancy and loss
concentration alongside frequency, not win rate alone. No new trading gate or
economic change is authorized by this report itself.
