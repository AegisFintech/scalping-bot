# Current trading evidence and historical replay scope — ISSUE-502

[Issue502](https://github.com/AegisFintech/scalping-bot/issues/502) fixes misleading
current-observation interpretation, without changing trading or activating a
research strategy. Live0.9 request/receipt/prompt were verified, while the normal
09:00–09:30Z review proposed unsupported-release conditions as the cause of25
provider WAITs. That reason came from the frozen0.8-only research baseline.
Historical replay unavailability is not evidence of a provider or execution block.

## Smallest complete correction

The frozen replay, independent scorer, approved oracle and original reports stay
unchanged. An additive `research-observation-scope-1.0` projection retains every
context's release denominator, with supported0.8 baseline decisions separately
from unsupported/unavailable contexts. It never widens replay eligibility to0.9
or applies old0.40 economics to the current release. Mixed releases stay visible;
unknown applicability does not become zero trades or a simulated WAIT decision.

New descriptive review1.1 registrations pin this additional module before
computation. The model receives actual execution-funnel decisions/reasons,
explicit historical applicability, role/coverage denominators and recorded window
outcomes. Legacy reconstruction counts, unsupported baseline directions/reasons
and replay-filtered closed statistics remain in the local original diagnostic but
are omitted from model input, preventing a research limitation masquerading as a
trading gate. The revised prompt states this distinction explicitly. This reduces
redundant diagnostic input; model budget, cadence and existing claims are unchanged.

Actual closed rows are independently projected from the typed journal regardless
of replay release support. Preserve separate release/currency groups, exclusive
close-time cutoff, exact Decimal sums and signed fees already included in net.
Identical repeated trade identities are deduplicated; conflicting identity,
release, currency or money rejects. Original closed rows outside the window remain
counted separately. These are close-time statistics, not complete intent-cohort
returns, an equity curve, causal exit evidence or profitability qualification.
Counterfactual fills/net remain null and all authority flags false.

The receiver binds the scope to the same diagnostic/finding/plan and packet/window
as the verified funnel, including context and window-close denominators. Old,
missing, malformed or mismatched scope is unavailable. `/learning` shows supported
versus unavailable baseline contexts and recorded window outcomes. CURRENT/STALE
labels remain controlled by the existing server clock. Outer failure clears both;
no stale applicability is inherited. Rendering uses textContent, not HTML injection.
The main trading dashboard is unchanged.
